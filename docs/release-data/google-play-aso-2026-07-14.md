## Launch objective

This production-access ASO pass keeps the brand name while adding the high-intent
`tap away` phrase to the English title. The short descriptions now lead with the
concrete value proposition: 100 arrow mazes, order planning, no timer, and heart
management. The full descriptions explain the rules before listing features and
avoid rankings, price claims, competitor names, testimonials, and keyword blocks.

Google Play limits used for validation:

- App title: 30 characters or fewer.
- Short description: 80 characters or fewer.
- Full description: 4,000 characters or fewer.
- Metadata must be accurate, readable, relevant, and free of excessive repetition.

Arrow Again: Tap Away Puzzle turns every board into a quick route-planning challenge.

Find an arrow with a clear path, tap it away, and open the route for the next move. Clear the entire arrow maze in the right order while protecting your hearts. There is no timer, so you can study each board and solve at your own pace.

HOW TO PLAY
- Find an arrow whose route is open
- Tap to send it off the board
- Avoid blocked moves that cost a heart

- 100 portrait levels for short breaks
