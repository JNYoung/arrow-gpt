Privacy Policy Draft

This draft is prepared for release readiness. Replace the ad platform identifiers and publisher details before store submission, but the support channel below is now live.

Information We Process

Arrow Again stores game progress, settings, lives, stars, and local gameplay state on the device or in browser storage. The game does not require an account for core play.

Advertising

Rewarded advertising may be offered for optional hints or revive flows. Advertising partners such as Google AdMob or Meta may process device identifiers, approximate location, ad interaction data, and diagnostics according to their own policies.

Sharing and Platform Data

When platform sharing is available, the game may pass a score message, level number, star count, and a game share image to the host platform. On Meta Instant Games, the game may read the platform player display name when provided by the SDK.

Analytics

The game may log gameplay events such as level start, level complete, rewarded ad request, rewarded ad completion, and rewarded ad failure. These events are used to improve difficulty and stability.

Your Choices

  • You can reset local game progress by clearing browser/site data or uninstalling the app.
  • You can decline optional rewarded ads and continue core gameplay where available.
  • You can request deletion of platform-linked data through the data deletion page.
