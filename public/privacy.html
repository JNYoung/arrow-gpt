Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to Arrow Again, the Arrow Again official website, and related app store review pages. Arrow Again is a mobile puzzle game that does not require a standalone account for core gameplay.

Information We Process

Arrow Again may store game progress, settings, lives, stars, level state, and similar gameplay information locally on your device or in browser storage. The app may also process device type, operating system version, language, approximate region, app version, crash diagnostics, and usage events needed to operate and improve the game.

Advertising

Arrow Again may show optional rewarded ads for hint, revive, or similar reward flows. Advertising partners such as Google AdMob may process device identifiers, advertising identifiers, ad interaction data, approximate location, fraud-prevention signals, and diagnostics according to their own privacy policies and consent requirements.

You can reset or limit advertising identifiers through your device privacy settings. You may also manage personalized advertising preferences through tools provided by the relevant advertising network.

Platform Sharing and Social Data

When platform sharing is available, the game may pass a score message, level number, star count, and a game share image to the host platform. On Meta Instant Games or similar platform surfaces, the game may read platform-provided profile information such as display name or app-scoped user ID when required for gameplay, sharing, or support.

Analytics and Diagnostics

The game may log events such as level start, level completion, rewarded ad request, rewarded ad completion, rewarded ad failure, crash reports, and performance diagnostics. These events are used to improve stability, difficulty balance, and reward flows.

How We Use Information

  • Provide, maintain, and improve game features.
  • Save local progress and user settings.
  • Display optional rewarded ads and prevent advertising abuse.
  • Respond to support, privacy, and review-team requests.
  • Comply with app store, advertising, platform, and legal requirements.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be shared with service providers, advertising networks, platform providers, analytics or diagnostics tools, or legal authorities when needed for the purposes described in this policy.

Children's Privacy

Arrow Again is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will take reasonable steps to review and delete it where required.

Your Choices

  • You can clear local progress by deleting app data, clearing browser site data, or uninstalling the app.
  • You can decline optional rewarded ads and continue core gameplay where available.
  • You can use device privacy controls to reset advertising identifiers or limit ad personalization.
  • You can request support or deletion of platform-linked data through the contact email below.

Data Retention

Local gameplay data remains on your device until you delete it. Support messages and platform-linked requests are retained only as long as reasonably necessary to respond, maintain records, resolve disputes, or meet legal obligations.

Changes

We may update this policy when the app, advertising setup, platform requirements, or laws change. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
