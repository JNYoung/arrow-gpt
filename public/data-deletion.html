Data Deletion Instructions

Arrow Again does not require a standalone account for core gameplay. Most progress data is stored locally on your device or browser.

Delete Local Progress

  • Web: clear site data for the Arrow Again domain in your browser settings.
  • Android/iOS: uninstalling the app removes local app storage on the device.

Meta/Facebook Data

If you played through a Meta/Facebook surface and want deletion of platform-linked gameplay data, email REPLACE_WITH_SUPPORT_EMAIL with your app-scoped user ID or a screenshot of your platform profile. We will confirm deletion status by email.

Advertising Data

Advertising identifiers and ad interaction data are managed by the relevant ad network and operating system controls. Use your device privacy settings and ad network account controls to reset or limit ad personalization.
